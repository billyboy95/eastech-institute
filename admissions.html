Admissions

Student selection, admission & enrolment

Fair, transparent selection aligned to the Institute admissions policy, with routes for school leavers, artisans, and working adults.

Entry routes

National Senior Certificate / equivalent

Programme-specific subjects (Mathematics, Physical Sciences, or Information Technology as required).

Artisan and TVET articulation

Nated, occupational, and EASTC Artisan College graduates may enter with credit where CAT rules allow.

RPL

Recognition of Prior Learning assesses workplace competence against programme outcomes. Fees and evidence packs are advised at application.

How applications work

  1. Submit an online application with certified documents.
  2. Admissions screens for minimum criteria and programme capacity.
  3. Where required, complete a placement or language assessment.
  4. Receive an offer, pay registration, and enrol.
  5. Eligible students are referred to EASTC Foundation for bursary support.
