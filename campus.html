The artisanal building

EASTECH Institute is housed in the artisanal building — the green-roof hall where marketing (Blessing) works. This gallery is that building and its workshops only.

Institute, not school, not Food Court

The Technical School is the white classroom blocks. EASTC Varsity is the blue industrial hall. The Food Court is the glass pavilion with the wooden sign. Those photographs belong on those sites — not here.

Institute programmes share the Maxwell Street campus (DHET 2019/FE07/D33) and use the artisanal workshops, welding bays, and labs shown below.
